## Decisions
- **One assignment = one person, one position, one examination day.** Attendance, allowance and CPD attach 1:1/1:n to it, so per-day counting is exact.
- **Assignment statuses**: draft → (pending_approval) → offered → awaiting_volunteer_confirmation → confirmed → completed/no_show; declined, waitlisted, reassigned, cancelled. Allowed transitions and actors are rows in `assignment_transitions`. Volunteers can only accept/decline/withdraw (via `respond_assignment`); if `assignment.confirmation_requires_approval` is on, acceptance waits for an approver. Reassignment keeps the original record.
- **Conflicts**: one live assignment per person per day; cross-event overlap detected by time; override needs an approving officer and a reason.
- **Eligibility gate** (`volunteer_blockers`): active account, verified profile, verified required documents, plus position rules (registered professional / verified, unexpired licence). Allowance stage adds TIN, verified bank account, allowance documents.
- **Attendance** status and verification are separate. Late is flagged automatically after `attendance.late_after_minutes`.
- **Allowance**: created only from verified attendance; amount from the most specific active rule (event > position > category) effective on the duty date; processing and payment statuses are separate; approver ≠ processor; partial payments; payments cannot exceed balance without an authorised exception with reason; payments are immutable (return with reason).
- **CPD**: `units = qualifying verified service days × units_per_day` from versioned `cpd_rules` (default 2, proposed rule — not validated as official). Requires registered professional, verified unexpired licence, CPD-eligible position, qualifying attendance, verified attendance and review/approval (two-step by default, different people). One calendar date counts once per volunteer by default. Corrections flag records (`needs_review`) rather than altering them. Totals are labelled "recorded by this portal".
